In the classroom

Content is designed for practical use in criminal investigations where digital assets appear in evidence, records, victim reporting, and case strategy.

Program overview

Designed for casework.

The curriculum helps investigators identify and investigate cryptocurrency activity in criminal matters, from foundational blockchain principles through investigative and evidentiary workflows.

Instruction is built around the needs of state, local, and federal teams responsible for preserving evidence, evaluating records, and developing defensible case narratives.

Content is continuously updated with input from working investigators, prosecutors, technical specialists, and subject-matter experts.

Areas of instruction

What the curriculum covers.

  • 01Fundamental principles of cryptocurrency and blockchain systems.
  • 02Legal and procedural considerations for records, seizure, and evidentiary handling.
  • 03Investigative techniques for identifying cryptocurrency activity in criminal cases.
  • 04Internet-based investigative methods relevant to cryptocurrency matters.
  • 05Case studies and practical exercises based on current investigative patterns.
